Why is the answer 5 seconds?

The cylinder is slipping, so v > Rω. Friction at the contact point slows translation and speeds up rotation until v = Rω. Find that moment.

Step 2 of 3

Setting up the equations

Two coupled motions: linear retardation and angular acceleration.

Goal: find the time t when the rolling condition v = ωR is first satisfied.

Linear motion

Friction (kinetic):
fk = μkN = μkmg
Newton's 2nd law:
−μkmg = ma  ⇒  a = −μkg
Velocity at time t:
v = v0 − μkg t

Rotational motion

Torque about center:
τ = fk · R = (μkmg) R
I = ½mR² ⇒ τ = Iα:
α = 2μkg / R
Angular velocity at t:
ω = v0/4R + (2μkg/R) t

Step 3 of 3

Solving for the time t

Apply the rolling condition v = ωR to the equations from Step 2.

1 v0 − μkg t = ( v0/4R + (2μkg/R) t ) · R
2 v0 − μkg t = v0/4 + 2μkg t
3 v0 − v0/4 = 2μkg t + μkg t
4 3v0/4 = 3μkg t
5 t = v0 / (4μkg)
Substitute the values
v0 = 49 m/s,  μk = 0.25,  g = 9.8 m/s²
t = 49 / (4 × 0.25 × 9.8)
t = 49 / 9.8
FINAL ANSWER
t = 5 seconds
