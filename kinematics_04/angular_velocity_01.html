Screen 02 · Defining Angular Velocity
Definitions

Average Angular Velocity

ω̄ = Δθ / Δt

Total angular displacement divided by total time interval.

Instantaneous Angular Velocity

ω = dθ / dt

Limit of average angular velocity as Δt → 0.

Screen 04 · Relation Between Linear & Angular Velocity
Key Relation

Linear ↔ Angular

v = r ω

v = linear (tangential) speed

r = radius (distance from axis)

ω = angular speed (rad/s)

Insight
All points on a rigid rotating body share the same ω, but points farther from the axis have larger linear speed v.

Screen 06 · JEE Main Practice — Angular Velocity
Question

Q1.

A particle moves in a circle of radius 0.5 m with a constant angular speed. It completes 30 revolutions per minute. The linear speed of the particle is approximately:

Figure: particle on a horizontal circle, radius r = 0.5 m
Solution
Step 1 · Convert RPM → rad/s
ω = 2π × (30/60) = π rad/s ≈ 3.14 rad/s
Step 2 · Apply v = r ω
v = 0.5 × π = π/2 m/s ≈ 1.57 m/s
Answer
(B)  1.57 m/s
Takeaway
Always convert revolutions to radians (1 rev = 2π rad). Then v = rω gives linear speed in m/s if r is in metres.
Screen 07 · JEE Main Practice — Angular Velocity
Question

Q2.

The angular position of a rotating disc is given by θ(t) = 2t³ − 3t² + 4 (in radians, t in seconds). The angular velocity of the disc at t = 2 s is:

θ(t) = 2t³ − 3t² + 4
💡 Hint: ω = dθ/dt
Solution
Step 1 · Differentiate θ(t)
ω(t) = dθ/dt = 6t² − 6t
Step 2 · Substitute t = 2 s
ω(2) = 6(4) − 6(2) = 24 − 12 = 12 rad/s
Answer
(C)  12 rad/s
Takeaway
Instantaneous ω is the time derivative of θ(t). For variable rotation, evaluate ω = dθ/dt at the required instant.
